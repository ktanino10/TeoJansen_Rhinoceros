The public version requested on2026-11-15 is **Ver.3.4**, with engineering ID `v3.4-a-m3-r1`.

## Scope and sourcing exception, recorded before adoption

- Integrate the tested side-entry nut seat only into the front and centre synchronizing gears.
  Retain the rear gear near the reducer output, original tooth counts and shaft spacing.
- Convert A/D leg pins to M3/smooth6mm metal, redesigning bosses and washer reliefs rather than scaling holes alone.
  P's M3 outer nut collided with AC, so retain P's original M2 structure rather than burying nuts in small windows.
- Even after reducing B bosses to13mm, an M6 washer hit the frame at40degrees.
  Retain B, C/E three-body joints, foot guides/rockers and idlers at4mm/M2.
  Do not weaken the frame, use specialist miniature washers or widen the leg stack to force conversion.
- Sengoku BSW6mm is M6x13x1.0, thicker than the old M4 washer.
  A/D get0.3mm face reliefs while preserving4.1mm layer pitch and foot positions.
  A15.4mm-boss candidate collided with the right frame at30degrees and was rejected.
  The13mm bosses have2.5mm bearing width; sleeves, not fragile outer rims, locate the washers radially.
- Final-position clearance alone did not admit the M3 A-root screw head through the old neck.
  Add a real6.2mm axial passage and side-load the8mm head washer.
  Feed M3x30 through the passage before installing the leg/crank unit in the machine.
  The channel and hex-bore circumscribed circles retain a nominal gap exceeding3.69mm.
- **Only the continuous20mm metal sleeve is a preferred-shop exception.**
  That M3 through-metal length was not verified at Akizuki/Sengoku.
  Two short sleeves would interrupt bending support and increase part count.
  Use existing-family domestic Hirosugi CE-320N:20mm, JPY26 excluding tax, minimum50,
  checked2026-11-15 at https://hirosugi.co.jp/shop/g/gCE-301N/ .
  Stock/shipping are unknown. Do not substitute a spliced, hand-precision-cut or plastic sleeve.
-8mm sleeves, M3 screws/nuts and M3/M6 washers use verified preferred-shop listings.
  Do not confuse the maker's minimum lot or the JPY900 trial amount with a whole-machine quotation.
- Re-solve with actual CAD masses, dimensions and reactions. Hair-dryer supply is an uncalibrated proxy,
  not proof of self-start, loaded speed or physical30cm walking.
